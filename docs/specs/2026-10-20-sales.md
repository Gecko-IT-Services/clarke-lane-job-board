# Sales (Levi only) — 20 Oct 2026

## Why
Levi has no sales tool apart from his quote calculator, and wants to grow monthly orders to his target
(the amount is his, typed into the board's settings; never in this repo). Philip: give him something smart to
follow up his outstanding quotes (active or dead), and use the customer base and order history, with Claude
Sonnet 5.5 composing emails to existing customers.

## What
- Tab **Sales** (`#sales`), role `md` only (nav, render guard and RLS `is_md()` on every table).
- Quotes: every item in Factory Jobs › Quotes (read only, `allQuotes`). Open = Status not Ordered, not a
  lost-like status, not marked lost on the board.
- Win history (`winStats`): quotes dated in the last 12 months that are won, lost or over 60 days old. Win rate
  by count and value, days to order (median, 80th percentile), the chance a quote still open at 0/7/14/30/60/90
  days is later ordered, and per-customer rates (smoothed). Under 15 decided quotes: a cautious general curve,
  and the page says so.
- Pipeline (`pipeline`): stage due / follow / fresh / booked / cold / dead; chance = curve × customer factor;
  likely = value × chance. Likely dead after max(45, 1.5 × the 80th percentile days to order). Opens on Live.
- Chase today (`chaseList`): due follow-ups, then follow/cold by likely value. Draft follow-up email (template,
  no AI) to the quote's contact as an Outlook draft; it books the next follow-up a week ahead. Log a call, follow
  up on a date, lost with a reason (Undo).
- The month (`monthGap`): booked = the board's orders by order date + quotes marked Ordered this month not yet
  orders; gap to the target; likely from live quotes; quotes needed = what is left after likely ÷ (win rate ×
  average won order).
- Customers (`customerBase`/`quietCustomers`): Xero route `history` (md only, read only): approved/paid sales
  invoices and credit notes for 24 months with lines. Quiet = 3+ orders and silent > max(42 days, 2 × usual
  gap); lapsed = 2+ orders and nothing for 180 days. What they buy = most-ordered line descriptions.
- AI emails: Edge Function `sales-ai` (md only), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, Philip's choice),
  `@anthropic-ai/sdk@0.131.0`, effort medium, JSON output { subject, greeting, paragraphs }, server-side
  fallback `"default"`. Facts only (`customerFacts`: names, items and dates, open quote numbers, Levi's angle;
  never money). Logged in `sales_emails` with tokens and cost; `SALES_AI_DAILY_LIMIT` (30). Levi edits the
  text, then it becomes an Outlook draft. Never sent by the board.
- Tables: `quote_follow`, `sales_actions`, `sales_emails`.
